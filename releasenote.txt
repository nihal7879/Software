1. BILLABLE AND NOT BILLABLE LECTURES                                      NEW
================================================================================

A class can now be taught and deliberately not charged - a make-up for one the
institute cancelled, a free session before an exam, a demo for a student still
deciding. Until now the only ways to not charge were to not record the class at
all, which loses the teacher's work and the student's attendance, or to record
it and then correct the balance by hand.

  - The choice is per STUDENT per class, not per lecture. A group class can be
    charged to four students and not charged for the fifth who sat in.

  - Billable is the default everywhere. Nobody has to remember the normal case.

  - Set it in three places:
      * the lecture entry sheet (teacher's own, and the admin's on a teacher's
        behalf) - below the Meet link, and on the phone above Save
      * the QR attendance inbox, per scan, BEFORE confirming - so a class is
        never charged first and corrected afterwards
      * the Teachers page, afterwards, one click per student, if it was got
        wrong in the moment

  - The real length of the class is still recorded. A free two-hour class reads
    as two hours on the statement, marked "Not billable" - not as a zero-hour
    class that looks like a mistake.

  - What counts billable hours only: the student's balance, the month columns,
    the hours pivot, the Excel month totals.
    What still counts every class: attendance, the last-attended date, and the
    teacher's own taught hours. The teacher did the work whoever paid for it.

  - A not-billable entry is tinted pale amber wherever entries are listed, and
    in the Excel statement its row is amber with the hours struck through and
    "Not billable" in the Charged column. The struck-through figure matters:
    without it somebody adds that column up, gets more than the month totals,
    and concludes the statement is wrong.

  - The word is "Not billable", never "Free". On a statement "free" reads as a
    discount the family was given, when all that is meant is that this class is
    not billed.

  Verified against the real data: 215 students, hours attended equals hours
  charged (13,773.61 h) - the change moved nobody's balance. 21 lecture tests
  and 12 QR tests passed; the Excel was generated and read back.

2. FINANCE
================================================================================

  - FIXED: "Unknown column 'request_note'" when saving a payment. The deployed
    backend was running code from before the fix.

  - FIXED: "Could not save - please check the fields." That message named no
    field and appeared even when the fault was the server's, sending the office
    hunting through a form that was perfectly valid. The screen now shows what
    the server actually said - the fields it refused by name, or, for a real
    fault, "The server could not save this. Nothing has been changed - please
    report this to support."

  - Edit Payment now says which student the payment belongs to, with the amount,
    the date and who paid, so there is no guessing when the drawer opens.

  - Editing or removing a payment goes to the super admin for approval. The
    office enters it as they always did and is told it was sent; nothing is
    written to the statement until it is approved.


================================================================================
3. APPROVALS AND NOTIFICATIONS
================================================================================

  - An admin sending anything for approval now raises a web push to the SUPER
    ADMIN ("Waiting for your approval"), and the same notice appears in the
    sending admin's own bell. A request nobody looks at is worse than no
    request: the office believes the change is made and the family's statement
    says otherwise.

  - Approving or rejecting tells the admin who asked. A rejection carries the
    super admin's own reason, because the queue is their screen alone and that
    reason had nowhere else to be read.

  - NEW PAGE: Notifications, in the admin sidebar. Tabs for Everything, Finance,
    New logins and Lectures, with a search. Each row shows the exact wording
    sent, who it went to, when, and whether it reached a device - "On 2 devices",
    "In their bell only", "Did not send" or "Push is off". The second of those
    is said plainly because push is opt-in per device and most people never turn
    it on; it is not a failure.

  - Web push for five events: a new registration (office and super admin), an
    approved registration (the student and their parents), an hours statement
    (the parents), a check-in (the teacher and the parents) and a check-out
    (the parents).

  - A bell for every role, which clears its own count when opened. It reads the
    same log the push is written to, so there is no second list and no second
    decision about who hears what.

  - Notifications are turned on per device, from the bell, and the app asks once
    after signing in rather than hiding it in Settings.

  - Nothing with a figure in it is ever put in a notification. It is read off a
    lock screen by whoever is holding the phone.

  - FIXED: using "Confirm 6" in the QR inbox discarded anything typed into those
    rows. A teacher who marked a class not billable and then used the bulk
    button would have had it charged after all.

4. FORGOTTEN PASSWORD
================================================================================

  - A code by email for admin, teacher, parent and student. The system makes the
    new password; the account must then choose its own on the next sign-in.

  - An account with no email on file is told to contact the institute admin,
    rather than being left at a dead end.

  - Changing a password signs every other device out.
