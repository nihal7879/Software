================================================================================
ROLES AND PERMISSIONS
================================================================================

THE FIVE ROLES
--------------
  superadmin  the owner. Everything an admin has, plus the money overview
              (revenue, pending fees, hours consumed, revenue trend, teacher
              workload), the Pivots reports, and the switches described below.
  admin       the office. Students, hours, fees, teachers, attendance,
              registrations. No money overview, no Pivots.
  faculty     a teacher. Their own students, their own lectures, their own
              attendance inbox, their own QR code.
  student     their own hours, fees, lectures, and QR check-in.
  parent      the same, for a child they are linked to.


HOW THE HIERARCHY WORKS
-----------------------
server/src/middleware/auth.ts:

  allows(roles, role) = roles.includes(role)
                        || (role === 'superadmin' && roles.includes('admin'))

So requireRole('admin') lets a super admin through as well. A page that must be
super admin only uses requireSuperAdmin, never a role list.

On the client the same rule is in App.tsx's Protected component, and the menu is
built per role in components/Layout.tsx.

IMPORTANT: hiding something in the menu is not security. Anything an admin must
not see is ALSO refused by the server - analytics.ts omits revenue and pending
fees from its response unless the caller is a super admin, and the pivot routes
are behind requireSuperAdmin. Do both, always.


THE PERMISSION SWITCHES
-----------------------
Some things belong to the super admin by default but the office sometimes needs
them. Those are switches the super admin can hand over and take back, on the
Permissions page (super admin menu).

Defined in server/src/utils/settings.ts:

  hours_add       add hours to a student statement
  hours_deduct    deduct hours from a student statement
  hours_edit      edit an hours entry already on a statement
  hours_delete    delete an hours entry from a statement
  ledger_adjust   change the credited, pending and discount figures

They are independent. Granting "add" does not grant "deduct". This is
deliberate: the office is often allowed to put hours on but never take them off.

Stored in app_settings as perm_<key> = '0' | '1', with the grant time and the
person in updated_at / updated_by. A permission can also be given until a
moment: perm_<key>_until holds 'YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS' in Dubai time, and past it
the permission simply reads as off. Nothing runs on a schedule - the end time is
checked on every read.

Adding a new permission:
  1. Add the key and its wording to PERMISSIONS in utils/settings.ts. The
     wording is used both on the switch and in the refusal message, so they can
     never drift apart.
  2. Guard the route with requirePermission('your_key') AFTER requireRole.
  3. On the client, useMay('your_key') to decide what the button does.
The Permissions page lists whatever the server returns, so it needs no change.


HOW THE CLIENT SHOWS A MISSING PERMISSION
-----------------------------------------
The button stays on screen with a padlock, and pressing it explains who to ask:
"Permission required: only the super admin can ... Ask them to switch it on in
Permissions."

Do not hide the button. An admin who cannot find a button assumes the system is
broken and calls the office; one who can see it locked understands it is a rule.

The server refuses the request regardless of what the screen shows.


THE STUDENT LOCK
----------------
app_settings.student_panel_locked, on the same Permissions page. While locked,
students sign in to a "locked for now" screen: their dashboard, hours, fees and
lectures are refused by the server with 423, but profile, password and QR
check-in stay open. A student sitting in the classroom must still be able to
record attendance even if the family owes fees.

Implemented by blockLockedStudents in utils/settings.ts, applied to the student
data routes. It affects students only - parents, teachers and admins read the
same routes untouched.
