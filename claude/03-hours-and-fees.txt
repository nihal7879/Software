================================================================================
HOURS AND FEES - the money model
Read this before changing anything that touches fees, hours or the statement.
================================================================================

THE IDEA
--------
A student buys a block of hours. Every lecture they attend consumes some of
them. What is left is their balance. Money and hours are two sides of the same
record: a payment creates a package of hours.

  fee_transactions   the money that arrived
  fee_packages       the hours it bought (transaction_id points back)
  lecture_attendees  the hours used, one row per student per lecture
  hours_adjustments  manual corrections, each its own dated entry


THE FORMULA
-----------
Defined once, in server/src/utils/hoursSummary.ts. Never re-derive it anywhere
else - if a figure is needed somewhere new, import these expressions.

  credited = SUM(package_hours) + SUM(discount_hours) + SUM(adjusted_hours)
             over ACTIVE packages
           + SUM(delta) from hours_adjustments (not deleted)

  consumed = SUM(hours_consumed) from lecture_attendees
             where the attendee row AND its lecture are both not deleted
             AND the attendee row is_billable

  hours_left = credited - consumed          (may be negative - never clamp it)

  fee_status = 'Trial'            if the student is a Trial
               'Payment Required' if hours_left <= 0
               'In Credit'        otherwise

Two details that look like bugs but are not:
  - hours_left is allowed to go negative. That is a student who has over-run
    their package, and the office needs to see it.
  - The lecture's is_deleted is checked as well as the attendee's. Deleting a
    lecture must give its hours back, or deleting changes nothing visible.


BILLABLE AND NOT BILLABLE                                   added 2026-10-26
-------------------------
lecture_attendees.is_billable, default TRUE. A class can be taught and
deliberately not charged: a make-up for one the institute cancelled, a free
session before an exam, a demo for somebody still deciding.

The flag is on the ATTENDEE row, not the lecture, because a group class can be
charged to four students and free for the fifth who was sitting in.

hours_consumed still holds the REAL length of the class. The hours are not
zeroed - the student was there and the teacher taught it. Only the balance
ignores them. So:

  the balance, the month columns, the hours pivot   billable rows only
  the statement line, attendance, last attended     every row, tinted amber
                                                    and marked "Not billable"
  the teacher's taught hours                        every row, unfiltered
                                                    (they did the work)

The word is "Not billable", never "Free" - on a statement "free" reads as a
discount the family was given, when all that is meant is that this class is not
billed. One pale amber (NOT_BILLABLE_ROW in components/BillableToggle.tsx, and
FREE_BG in lib/hoursStatementExcel.ts) marks it on every screen and in the
Excel, so it is recognised without being read.

Where a screen adds up a list of lectures itself, it must use chargedHours()
from api/client.ts and never Number(l.hours_consumed) - otherwise the months
stop adding up to the balance. The server side is already decided in
CONSUMED_EXPR and nowhere else.

Set when the lecture is recorded (the sheet, and the QR inbox before confirming
- lecture_checkins.is_billable carries the choice onto the lecture). Corrected
afterwards, per student, with
  PATCH /lectures/:id/attendees/:studentId/billable
which moves the balance by the length of that lecture and is audited as
SET_BILLABLE with both sides. It is NOT behind the approval queue, for the same
reason a lecture entry is not: recording the class is the teacher's job, and
they already decide its length.


WHY IT IS WRITTEN AS SUBQUERIES
-------------------------------
There is a student_hours_summary VIEW in the schema. Do not use it on any path
that serves a page. It re-aggregates the whole of lecture_attendees,
fee_packages and hours_adjustments on every read. The expressions in
hoursSummary.ts are correlated subqueries keyed on student_id, so the cost is
proportional to the rows returned, not to the size of the tables.


HOURS COME FROM ONE PLACE ONLY
------------------------------
Hours are credited by recording a payment in Finance. Nothing else may create
them.

This rule was learned the hard way. The Trial-to-Enrolled dialog used to have a
"first package hours" box that wrote a fee_packages row with no payment behind
it and nothing in Finance. The office then also recorded the real payment, which
credited the same hours a second time. Three students were double-credited by 75
hours in total before it was found. The box is gone from both the dialog and the
server. Do not add anything like it back.

A package with transaction_id = NULL is therefore suspicious. The only
legitimate ones are trial hours, which are genuinely free.


MANUAL ADJUSTMENTS
------------------
hours_adjustments is the correct way to correct a balance: one row per
correction, with a delta, a reason, and adjusted_on.

adjusted_on is the day the adjustment is FOR, which is usually not the day it
was typed. "Adjusted till 16 May", entered in September, belongs on the
statement in May - otherwise the running balance reads wrong from May onwards.
Left empty it falls back to when it was entered.

Editing or removing an adjustment marks it, it does not erase it, and each of
those four actions is behind its own permission - see 04-roles-and-permissions.


FEE IMPORT
----------
Bank statements are imported into fee_import_drafts. A row that matches a
student with a valid amount and date becomes a real transaction straight away;
anything else waits in the drafts list for the office to assign. Each draft
carries a flag - new, already recorded, or already in drafts - computed per read
so a double entry is visible before it is assigned.

The draft UI (client/src/pages/admin/Finance.tsx) is the pattern the QR
attendance inbox copies: a card per row, the immutable imported facts on top,
the fields to fill underneath, the action buttons at the bottom right.


WHEN CHANGING ANY OF THIS
-------------------------
Work out the before and after figures for the affected students and print both.
Every script in server/scripts/ that touched money does this. A change to the
formula that nobody verified against real students is not finished.
